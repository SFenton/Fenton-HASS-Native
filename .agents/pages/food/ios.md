# Food & Recipes: iPhone and iPad

> **What:** native Food implementation notes. **Read when:** changing `HassUI/Features/Food` or `HassCore/Recipes`.

- Service: `EverShelfRecipeService` (HassCore) over `HassConnection.callServiceWithResponse`, which sends websocket `call_service` with `return_response: true` and returns `response`. Parsing (`RecipeParsing`) ports `recipeTypes.ts`: snake or camel keys, loose numbers and flags, strict ingredient keys, bounded lists; an inconsistent group drops grouping rather than mis-grouping. Inventory reuses the transport.
- Routing: `FoodRoute` (`hub`, `recipes`, `inventory(InventorySpace)`) with `.foodNavigation()` on a `NavigationStack`; Home registers it, other lanes push `FoodRoute.hub`. Debug launch `FENTON_OPEN_FOOD=hub|recipes`. HIG: Navigation (push for hierarchy, sheet for a focused task).
- The space and All Food tiles open the Inventory lane's pages (`FoodInventoryScreen` → `InventoryDestinationScreen`, models from `inventoryModels` or the connection); `HomeRouteDestination.food` opens the hub (`FoodEntryScreen`).
- Hub links and tiles have their own action that sets the hub's `pushed` route (`.navigationDestination(item:)` → `FoodNavigation.destination`). Direction G (n6 Food & Recipes): All Recipes and All Food are `FoodLinkRow`s (a one-row `ModuleList` of a `ModuleRow` with a chevron; the identifier sits on the button); the spaces are neutral off `GlassTile`s (places have no colour family) in an equal-height grid; "Suggested Recipes" is a `PageSectionHeader` with "Page 1 of 5" trailing (`FoodSection(detail:)`, drawn by the carousel so it follows the scroll). A tile drawn with `.allowsHitTesting(false)` inside a `NavigationLink` label ignored every tap on iPhone ([entity-tile](../../patterns/entity-tile.md) on the Rooms branch). `testHubTilesOpenTheirPages` taps them.
- Hub: one column; two (suggestions + All Recipes, All Food + spaces) where `SplitPolicy` allows. The carousel is a paged `ScrollView` with page dots VoiceOver can adjust. HIG: Collections, Page controls.
- Recipes: "Sorted by Ingredients Available" under the large title (`RecipeGrid.sortSummary`), then `RecipeGrid` on `DynamicGridMetrics` (160 pt min, 2 to 8 columns) of `ImageCard`s; shared floating actions and `FilterSheet` ([floating-actions](../../patterns/floating-actions.md)). Results and hydration are announced. HIG: Searching, Sheets.
- Primary buttons (Retry, Open in Cookidoo on Instructions, Add Missing Ingredients) are G's `PrimaryButton`/`PrimaryButtonStyle` (`#0071E3`), one per screen; Open Source Recipe on General is a list row in the action tint (`RecipeLinkRow`). `FoodStyle.action` is gone.
- Accessibility sizes: `ImageCard` becomes a row (72 pt thumbnail beside the title); fact rows drop the glyph and put the value under the label (`RecipeFactRow`); the filter's horizon becomes a menu. HIG: Typography (prioritize text at large sizes).
- The split pane has its own header (title, Close) instead of a `NavigationStack`: a stack nested in the Food stack's Recipes page showed nothing on iPad landscape. The hub keeps the open recipe's `RecipeDetailModel` in `@State`; built in the sheet closure, live entity updates rebuilt it and reset the tab. The hub carousel clips its pages in split columns (unclipped, page two drew under the inventory column).
- Debug: `FENTON_FOOD_FIXTURES=1` gives the signed-in app's Food pages the fixture service; `FENTON_FOOD_DEBUG=1` shows the open detail model's identity and tab (`recipe.debug`) for journeys. They separated live data from the app when the iPad sheet ignored taps (cause: the hero photo's hit area, see image-card).
- Detail: a `.large` sheet with the circle-x Close on single-page layouts, "8/9 ingredients" as the iOS 26 subtitle (`sheetSubtitle`); where a split is allowed (iPad landscape, unfolded Duo landscape) the trailing half shows it with Close, the open card is outlined and marked selected, and folding moves it into the sheet. Tabs are the bottom glass `modalTabBar` (`recipe.tabs`). Facts, ingredient groups, equipment and steps are `ModuleList`s (`#2C2C2E` cells under `modalChrome()`; the split pane sets `tileSurface` to `.sheet`; glyph-less lists pass `hasGlyphs: false`; facts are `ModuleRow`s; chips wrap in HassDesign's `FlowLayout`) under 15 Semibold secondary headers (`RecipeSectionHeader`); ingredient marks are a green check, a question mark (uncertain match) or an empty circle. HIG: Segmented controls, Split views.
- Symbols: Food maps its MDI names locally (`FoodSymbols`) instead of extending `SymbolMap`, whose duplicate keys crash at launch.
- Gallery (dark, on the house backdrop): `FENTON_GALLERY=food`, `FENTON_GALLERY_ROUTE=hub|recipes`, `FENTON_GALLERY_FOOD_MODE=error|empty|slow`; 120 fixture recipes (every fourth Cookidoo), recorded calls in `gallery.lastCall`.
  The debug "Last call" line sits bottom-leading in a 180 pt chip (a full-width bar covered the floating actions; a top inset covered the back button) and is hidden at accessibility sizes (it clipped and covered the page). Fixture sorts break ties by id, so by availability the grid leads with 1, 6, 11; journeys open 6 and check 11 beside the pane.
- Tests: `RecipeParsingTests`, `RecipeGroceryPolicyTests`, `RecipeFormattingTests`, `RecipeServiceTests`, `FoodConfigTests` (HassCore); `FoodModelTests`, `FoodViewTests`, `FoodStateTests` (HassUI; scripted service for failures, paging, hydration and grocery outcomes; `RecipesScreen`/`FoodHubScreen` take initial `WindowMetrics` and selection so hosted renders reach the split); `FoodJourneyTests` (devices, audits, screenshots).
