# Chores, Groceries and To-Do (iPhone and iPad)

> **What:** native structure and departures for the todo pages. **Read when:** changing `HassUI/Features/Chores` or `HassCore/Todo`.

- `ChoresScreen` (tab) and `AdminTodoScreen` (Settings row, hidden from Steph by the cached viewer id) host a `ChoresModel` (service, shared `TodoStore`, viewer id cached in `UserDefaults` `chores.viewerUserId` and refreshed from `auth/current_user`). Sub-pages push with `navigationDestination(for: ChoresConfig.Page.self)`, which hands the `ChoresModel` on explicitly: a pushed page's environment comes from the section's `NavigationStack`, above `ChoresModelHost`, and without it every quick link crashed (2026-10-13).
- `TodoStore.shared(for:)` keeps one `TodoListController` per list for the connection, so lists keep their items across pages, and `TodoItemsCache` (Application Support `TodoItems.json`, file-protected, per server) shows the last items on a cold launch before the first fetch. Sign-out clears it and the viewer id.
- Every visible list loads from a zero-size background view, drawn or not, so a hidden list reappears when its entity count rises.
- Add is the shared floating action bar ([floating-actions](../../patterns/floating-actions.md)); HIG: Buttons. Sheets are `NavigationStack` + `Form` with Cancel / confirm in the bar (HIG: Sheets); the DoneTick form opens at the large detent, the one-field sheets at medium. The add sheet shows Task Name and Description as two sections, each with a `FormHeader` and a `FormFooter` saying what goes in it, and no "Donetick" subtitle (owner, 2026-10-16).
- Rows (owner, 2026-10-13; updated 2026-10-16): no card behind a row. The check circle is **the size of the title** (owner, 2026-10-16: it was bigger): it takes `.font(.body)` at the symbol's default scale (it had `.imageScale(.large)`) on a first-baseline `HStack`, so it follows Dynamic Type with the title and centers on the title's first line, inside a 24 pt `@ScaledMetric` column that keeps the title on the module's hairline inset. The hit target is the whole row (52 pt, 60 pt with a due line), far over 44 pt. The edit glyph is `square.and.pencil` labelled "Edit {title}" on every page (it was `info.circle`; HIG SF Symbols).
- **The page is a `List`** (2026-10-16), not a `ScrollView` of `ModuleList`s: swipe actions only exist on `List` rows before iOS 27, and Inventory already does the same. `TodoPageView` is an inset-grouped `List` whose plain rows (quick links, headers, errors, the empty state) draw no card (`TodoPlainRow`) and whose task rows draw their share of the module with `ModuleRowBackground` (dense when the section has an overdue row) and a hairline from `moduleRowSeparator` (`HassDesign/ModuleList.swift`). One implicit `motionSafeAnimation` on the list, keyed by `TodoPageLayout.animationKey`, animates a row away when it is completed or deleted and back in on rollback (fade under Reduce Motion).
- **Swipe design** (owner, 2026-10-16; HIG, Lists and tables: the row's primary action leads, secondary and destructive actions trail, as in Mail and Reminders): leading swipe (left to right in LTR) is **Complete** (or Mark Not Done), green, full swipe performs it; trailing swipe is **Delete** (outermost, red, full swipe asks "Delete {title}?" through a confirmation dialog because the removal is irreversible) then **Edit** (blue). SwiftUI's `.leading` and `.trailing` edges mirror in right-to-left layouts, so the order flips with the language. Every swipe action is also in the row's context menu (destructive last, HIG Context menus) and is a VoiceOver custom action on the row's toggle (Edit, Delete; Complete is the toggle itself). `TodoRowActions` is the one source for all three, so they cannot disagree. Fills are the darker app fills (green `#2A732E`, blue `#0071E3`, red `#D93025`) so the white label keeps 4.5:1. Complete goes through `TodoListController.toggle` (its optimistic statuses, the todo list's own revert timer, rather than `OptimisticActions`, which tracks single entity states); Delete is `TodoListController.remove` (row leaves at once, returns with the error on failure).
- **Quick-link tiles** (owner, 2026-10-16): filled tiles with a white filled glyph, one colour per page from `ColorFamily` (`choreStephen` indigo `#4C3CCB`, `choreSteph` fuchsia `#A8209A`, `choreUnassigned` mustard `#705600`, `choreHome` steel slate `#4A5A70`, `groceries` cocoa `#6E4430`; a link keeps its colour whoever is signed in). The web's chore colours (`ha-sfenton-react-dash@f5a7bf9:src/constants/portedDashboard.ts:198-204`: groceries rust `#9B4348`, Stephen teal `#006078`, Steph orange `#D46C00`, Unassigned purple `#513A7E`, Home teal `#408F9A`) collide with device families (cooling and air teal, heating orange, cleaning purple, media pink and away red), so they are not used; the new fills are each at least 0.06 apart in OKLab from every device and security fill (`ColorFamilyTests.choreTilesUseColoursNothingElseDoes`) and keep 4.5:1 for the white title, the 92% subtitle and the status-chip lift.
- Departures: quick links use the glass tile face with the web color on the icon and border rather than a 72% color fill (text contrast on the material, HIG: Materials); due tones put the color on a symbol and keep label-colored text (4.5:1 on the material); due date and time are optional toggles with native pickers instead of empty date fields; Specific Days of Week is a checkmark list instead of a multi-select box.
- Split view on iPad and unfolded Duo landscape (quick links primary, sub-page secondary) waits for the navigation shell's `OnDemandSplitStack` (PR #3); until then sub-pages push full width everywhere.
- Debug gallery: `FENTON_GALLERY=chores` (+ `FENTON_GALLERY_TODO_PAGE=<route>`, `FENTON_GALLERY_VIEWER=steph`, `FENTON_GALLERY_EMPTY=1`) with a fixture service that applies check-offs, adds, renames and deletes.
