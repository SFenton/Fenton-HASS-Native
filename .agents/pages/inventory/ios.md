# Inventory: iPhone and iPad

> **What:** native implementation notes for the inventory pages, item editor and Add Item sheet. **Read when:** changing `HassCore/Inventory` or `HassUI/Features/Inventory`.

- **Services.** `EverShelfClient` (`HassCore/Inventory`) wraps every EverShelf service the pages call over the one `HassConnection` websocket. Services with responses go through `HassServiceResponding.callServiceForResponse` (`call_service` with `return_response: true`); `prepare_scanned_product` is response-only, the others are optional, `todo.add_item` has none. Home Assistant owns every change: actions call the service, then re-read the page.
- **Models.** `InventoryListModel` per page, kept for the session in `InventoryModels` so going back shows the last rows (no reload on reopen); `refreshLoaded()` after an add. `InventoryEditorModel` and `ScanItemModel` port `useInventoryItemDetails` and `ScanItemCameraSheet`. Pure rules (grouping by lowercased name, batches by location/expiry/prepared, decreases smallest row first, filters and sorts with undated last) live in `InventoryCatalog`; dates are judged on the local calendar (`InventoryCalendar`), not EverShelf's UTC `days_remaining`.
- **Prepared food** shows the expected batches at once, then re-reads the inventory unfiltered every 1 s until it matches or 10 s pass (web `PREPARED_REFRESH_RETRY_MS` / `PREPARED_OPTIMISTIC_REVERT_MS`).
- **Navigation.** `InventoryRoute.space(_)` with `.inventoryDestinations()` on a stack. The Food & Recipes hub pushes these; until it lands the Home stack registers them and debug builds push `FENTON_OPEN_INVENTORY=<space>` at launch.
- **Page.** Direction G (mockups n6-inventory, n6-inventory-empty): a large title with a "4 Items • 0 Expiring Soon" line (the Food hub's `FoodConfig.summary` wording over the page's cards; hidden while searching), then the rows on one dense module (orange and red expiry text). The `List` stays (swipe actions need it before iOS 27): each row draws its share of the module (`InventoryRowBackground`, 26 pt corners on the first and last rows) and is marked `// G: ModuleList`. A row is the name (17), its subtitle (15, secondary, or orange with a clock and red with a triangle), a 34 pt grey cart circle in a 44 pt target and, when it opens the editor, a chevron; the whole row opens the editor, and delete is the trailing swipe action (single batches) or the editor's Delete. Rows switch to a stacked layout at accessibility sizes (`ViewThatFits`). Search, Sort, Filter and Scan Item are the shared `floatingActionBar` (Scan Item is a titled glass capsule on an empty page) and `FilterSheet` with a "4 of 41 Items" subtitle ([floating-actions](../../patterns/floating-actions.md)). Quantity prompts are alerts with a field; single deletes confirm. Sort Direction is two option rows with the web's hint as subtitle (a segmented control and footnote clipped at AX sizes). Empty and error states (`InventoryEmptyState`: basket, 22 Bold title, secondary description, Try Again as the `PrimaryButton`) are wrapping text centred in a scroll view, not `ContentUnavailableView` in a list row. HIG: Lists and tables, Alerts, Action sheets.
- **Editor.** Direction G edit sheet (mockup n6-item-editor): circle x Cancel, the filled check saves every changed batch in turn (`saveChanges()`, the same calls each batch's own Save made; it closes when all are done), "Quantity 3 · In the pantry" as the iOS 26 subtitle. One group per batch headed by its expiry and place (`FormHeader`): quantity `Stepper` (max 999), optional expiry (toggle + `DatePicker`), prepared toggle (asks how many above one, applies at once), a red Delete row with a trailing trash; the save hint and the Prepared Food note in the footer; Reset Changes as an action-tint row. A sheet on iPhone, folded Duo and portrait; in the trailing half where `SplitPolicy` allows (iPad landscape, unfolded Duo landscape) with Close and a Save Changes row, since the pane has no toolbar. Saves and deletes block dismissal.
- **Add Item.** Three steps in one sheet (mockups n6-scan-item, n6-scan-review): circle x Cancel on step 1, a Back circle on later steps, the filled check adds on review and Close shows once added; the step subtitle is the iOS 26 navigation subtitle (in the form before). The camera is a 4:5 card with 26 pt corners and a status pill ("Looking for a Barcode" with an arc); manual entry sits under it in the action tint; one footer button at most is the filled `PrimaryButton`, the other a grey capsule. Review is a product header, one group (Name, How Many as the typed number plus a stepper, expiry, Prepared Food) and the "Where should it be stored?" checkmark list; the step's buttons sit below the form in a stack (as a bottom safe-area inset they covered the last row on short screens), and inside the form at accessibility sizes. The camera is AVFoundation (`AVCaptureMetadataOutput` for EAN-13/8, UPC-E, Code 128/39, ITF-14; `AVCapturePhotoOutput` for the date, downscaled to 1600 px JPEG) instead of the spec's VisionKit DataScanner, which can't capture a still for `read_expiry_image`. Simulators have no camera: the sheet says so and offers manual entry. Debug `FENTON_SCAN_CAMERA=simulated` draws a viewfinder with Simulate Scan (barcode `FENTON_GALLERY_BARCODE`, default the fixture's Greek Yogurt) for journeys and screenshots. `NSCameraUsageDescription` is in `project.yml`.
- **Colors.** Expired and soon tones are G's `HassTokens.Ink.error` and `.warning` (4.5:1 on the dense module and in sheets); an icon carries the tone too. Success keeps its own green.
- **Screenshots.** Under `FENTON_REDACT_PRIVATE=1` the live route redacts item names (`privacySensitive`); gallery fixture rows stay readable.
- **Gallery and tests.** `FENTON_GALLERY=inventory` on `EverShelfFixtureServer` (synthetic rows), with `FENTON_GALLERY_SPACE`, `FENTON_GALLERY_OPEN` (`sort`, `filter`, `scan`, `editor:<name>`), `FENTON_GALLERY_STATE` (`empty`, `error`, `loading`), `FENTON_GALLERY_HOLD_ADDED=1` (keeps the shopping check 30 s for journeys). Journeys: `InventoryJourneyTests` (fixture) and `testLivePantryOpensFromHome` (signed in, `FENTON_OPEN_INVENTORY=pantry`).
