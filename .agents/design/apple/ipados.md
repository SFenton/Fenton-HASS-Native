# iPadOS

> **What:** iPad navigation decisions. **Read when:** building for regular width.

- Navigation: the overlay flyout from FestivalNativeApps (slides over content with a scrim, never resizes content), not a persistent sidebar. Built in `HassUI/App/Shell/` (`NavigationFlyout`, `ShellNavigator`, `ShellNavigationStyle`):
  - Regular width (iPad, unfolded Duo, any orientation) hides the root `TabView`'s bar and shows **Open Navigation** (`nav.open`, `line.3.horizontal`) on each section root; a leading-edge swipe opens it while the section is at its root. Compact width (iPhone, folded Duo) keeps the tab bar (web `bottom` layout). The style follows the size class only, so rotation never swaps the navigation model.
  - Rows follow the web rail/drawer (`AdaptiveNavigation.tsx`): heading "Navigation", Home, Security, Climate, Chores, then Settings pinned at the bottom (scrolls with the rows at large text sizes). Only Chores carries a badge (`ShellNavigator.overdueChores`, web `BADGED_NAV_PATH`), spoken as "Chores, N overdue".
  - Closes on a destination, scrim tap, Escape (`.cancelAction`) or the VoiceOver escape gesture. Opening focuses the heading; closing without a destination returns focus to Open Navigation.
  - The content is hidden from VoiceOver while it is open, on three layers (verified on iPad 2026-10-11; one layer alone left elements in the audit): `accessibilityHidden(while:)` on the `TabView`, the same on every page through `.pageChrome()` (SwiftUI vends a page's elements past UIKit), and `accessibilityElementsHidden` on the root `UITabBarController` view (the UIKit navigation bar's large title is out of SwiftUI's reach). Use `accessibilityHidden(while:)` (`isEnabled:` form), never `.accessibilityHidden(bool)`.
  - The scrim is two layers: a full-window dim that neither hit-tests nor reaches VoiceOver, and the **Close Navigation** target (`nav.flyout.scrim`) beside the panel only. A full-window close button won the accessibility hit test over the rows. The dim leaves the status bar alone (dimmed, its window title read below 3:1).
  - The selected row is primary ink on an accent tint (`accentColor` at 18%), like Settings' selected row; white on the accent read 2.5:1. Rows have vertical padding so they grow with Dynamic Type.
  - The leading-edge swipe (`flyoutEdgeSwipe`, decided by `ShellNavigator.edgeSwipeEnded`) is a UIKit `UIScreenEdgePanGestureRecognizer` through `UIGestureRecognizerRepresentable` (iOS 18+; iOS 17 and macOS fall back to a simultaneous drag). Once it recognizes it cancels the touch in the content, so a swipe that starts on a control opens only the flyout; a SwiftUI simultaneous drag also opened the Home camera tile under it on the landscape Duo. Never a hit-testable edge strip: it swallowed taps on Open Navigation and on row edges.
  - On the unfolded Duo the system moves Open Navigation into its trailing vertical bar, and the bar covers the trailing ~83 pt (taps there never reach the app).
  - Every page in a section stack applies `.pageChrome()` (pushed pages too) so the tab bar stays hidden in flyout style; section roots also apply `.sectionRootChrome()`.
  - Departure from the web: the web shows a persistent 248 px rail at ≥1120×820; the native app follows the owner's FestivalNativeApps verdict (persistent sidebars looked bad; overlay flyout instead).
- Rotates freely. Portrait is always single page; landscape may split.
- Splits follow [split-view.md](split-view.md): on demand, landscape regular width only, 50/50 at the midpoint.
- Grids grow columns with width through `DynamicGridLayout` (web UX contract). Resolved 2026-10-15 (plan Phase 4, decision 1): direction G's "4 columns" is a floor, not a ceiling. A full-width tile grid reaches at least 4 columns at iPad Pro 11 portrait (content about 802 pt) and keeps growing; caps of 3 on tile grids are gone (Chores, Vacuums, Household settings), and the one remaining bound is Chores quick links at 4 (five links, one two tracks wide). Room pages and Media show 2 + 2 tiles across by laying their sections side by side. See [dynamic-grid](../../patterns/dynamic-grid.md).
- Status chips (Home, Security, room pages) wrap onto rows in regular width and scroll sideways in compact width (`ChipRail`, [status-rail](../../patterns/status-rail.md)); nothing in regular width hides behind a scroll.
- **Quick Links is a floating glass circle** on section roots in regular width (iPad and the unfolded Duo; mockup n1-ipad-home): 56 pt, bottom trailing, 16 pt from the trailing edge and 8 pt above the home indicator, `navigationGlass(in: Circle())` (Liquid Glass on iOS 26, `.ultraThinMaterial` earlier). `FloatingQuickLinksButton` in `Features/Rooms/QuickLinksSheet.swift`; `ShellChrome.showsFloatingQuickLinks` is true exactly where the flyout style is. It is a bottom safe-area inset, so scroll content can scroll clear of it and the system's trailing bar on the unfolded Duo stays clear. Identifier `quickLinks.open`, VoiceOver label "Quick Links", the same as the toolbar button it replaces; the toolbar button remains on pushed room pages, and the flyout keeps its Quick Links row. HIG: Toolbars and Materials (Liquid Glass belongs to the navigation layer), Buttons (44 pt minimum target).
- **Sheets name their size.** `.hassSheet(.page)` for viewing and control sheets with tile grids, dials, maps or a second column; `.hassSheet(.form)` for lists, editors and prompts; the default is `.form`. There is no `.automatic`. HIG: Sheets, "Prefer page or form sheet styles in an iPadOS app". See [modal-sheet](../../patterns/modal-sheet.md).
