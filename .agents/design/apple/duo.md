# iPhone Duo

> **What:** foldable iPhone adaptation. **Read when:** changing layout that a fold or hinge can cross.

- Rotates freely folded and unfolded (`FentonOrientationSupport` allows all orientations once `HingePresence` sees a hinge or vertical bar). Folded and unfolded portrait are single page; unfolded landscape may split ([split-view](split-view.md)).
- Carry over FestivalNativeApps' Duo rules: on the inner display the on-demand split divider sits on the hinge in every pose (the active fold in book pose, the inactive hinge region while flat; see [split-view](split-view.md)); the outer display behaves as an iPhone.
- Run the `apple-hig` skill's `/duo` workflow stage by stage before Duo work.
- **The hinge in layout** (plan Phase 4, 2026-10-15). `EnvironmentValues.windowHinge` (HassDesign; the shell reads it once, `readsWindowHinge()`) is the one source. Every rule below is geometric (a hinge rect), never a device-name check, and is unit tested by feeding a rect measured on the simulator (hinge band 455.3 to 495.7 pt in a 951 pt window, content ending at the trailing bar's 868 pt).
  - **Even columns.** A `DynamicGridLayout` rounds its column count down to an even number (at least two) while a hinge is set, so no track pair straddles the fold ([dynamic-grid](../../patterns/dynamic-grid.md)).
  - **Chip gap.** A `ChipRail` (Home, Security, room pages) wraps in regular width and stops 24 pt short of the hinge (`ChipRailMetrics.hingeGap`, direction G: "the chip row stops 24pt short of the hinge"); the system's trailing vertical bar also covers about 83 pt and sits in the safe area.
  - **Two-column Home.** In unfolded landscape Home splits on the hinge (`HomeColumns`, built on `SplitPolicy.geometry`): chips and weather lead, cameras and guests trail, the hinge band stays empty (mockup n1-duo-unfolded). Cameras take the 2 x 2 layout in the trailing half. iPad landscape stays one column (n1-ipad-home), and unfolded portrait is one column.
  - **Quick Links and Profile** are header buttons, not a floating circle (owner, 2026-10-16; reshaped 2026-10-18, option A, [direction-g](../direction-g.md) Navigation). On the unfolded Duo the system moves bar items into its trailing vertical bar; the folded Duo's vertical rail does too. Measured on the folded Duo simulator (iOS 27.1, 2026-10-18): Quick Links (a plain symbol item) goes into the rail and is found there as a button, while the Profile monogram, a custom view, **stays in the horizontal top bar** (a custom view cannot go vertical; FestivalNativeApps avoids that by showing the symbol `person.crop.circle.fill` in the vertical bar, S+H keeps the monogram on the owner's mockup, "on Duo the avatar stays in the horizontal bar"). The disc is still 44 pt. The header adds no `ToolbarSpacer(.fixed)` in a vertical bar (`shellInVerticalBar`, set from `toolbarVerticalEdge`; HIG Designing for iPhone Duo: "system spacing adapts, so don't add fixed spacing").
  - Verified on the `iPhone Duo (HASS)` simulator unfolded in landscape (2026-10-16): Home splits with the chips stopping short of the hinge, the weather card on compact paging (its hourly row pages by the compact count in the half-width column, nine hours squeezed 400 pt), cameras 2 x 2 in the trailing half and the Quick Links circle bottom trailing (`AdaptiveLayoutJourneyTests.testHomeSplitsOnTheHingeOnlyWhenUnfoldedInLandscape`). Unfolded portrait and the partly folded pose were not run.
  - **A rule for anything that reads the hinge:** measure the full-width frame *outside* any padding that depends on the hinge. `ChipRail` first measured inside its own trailing padding, so the inset shrank the frame it was computed from, flipped every layout pass and pinned the main thread at 100% CPU on the unfolded Duo (journeys timed out with "Unable to perform work on main run loop"). The unit tests cannot see that; only the unfolded simulator did.
- Window changes (fold, unfold, rotate) move an open detail between the trailing pane and a push, one run-loop turn after the window update, unanimated.

## Duo rail (owner, 2026-10-20)

No hamburger on the Duo (folded or vertical rail). In the vertical rail Quick Links and Profile sit in one shared glass group (`ToolbarItemGroup`, Profile as the person symbol, no fixed spacer (owner, 2026-10-20; a monogram custom view stays in the horizontal bar, so the rail group shows the symbol)), replacing the two circles of 2026-10-18. See [direction-g](../direction-g.md).
