Motion timings the owner will feel

Six motion findings from the atlas review change what a person sees or feels, so they wait for a pick. Each shows today (left) beside the proposal (right); press Play. The no-visible-change fixes (tokens for the segment glide and panel fade, completion chaining of the arrival, the duplicate reset, the app tile ring cross-fade, the spring spelling) are already built and tested on this branch.

1 · The login mark's rise is hidden behind the header

Cause. LoginView applies .opacity(stage.title) to the whole header (0 until 0.46 s), so the mark's 0.86→1.03 spring (0.15–0.43 s) plays at opacity 0 and only the settle shows. motion.md board 1 says "glow → mark springs in → title → form". Same cause on cold launch signed out and on the session-expired welcome-back.

5 · Possible flash between layers on session expiry

Cause (from code, not confirmed on a device). MainTabView leaves with .transition(.identity) while the sign-in layer fades in over a root with no ground of its own (RootView.swift:140–158). Proposed: the root paints the launch ground, so the fade is dark→sign-in, never white. Needs a frame capture before it is called a bug; I would build it only if the capture shows a flash.
