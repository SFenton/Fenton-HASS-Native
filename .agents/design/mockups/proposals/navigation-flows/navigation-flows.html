Navigation flows: four owner choices

The HIG navigation review of the atlas (#144) left four findings whose fix changes what a person sees or feels. Seven others were fixed in code with tests (link landing, duplicate push, page chrome at the stack, Rooms order, Vacation destinations). Per propose-first nothing below is built. Every section shows the stack or flow today beside the options; they are structural, so they are drawn as stacks and timelines rather than pixel mockups, and the same applies on iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad (the rule is geometric; no device differs except where noted). Text size changes nothing here: no copy or layout moves.

3. Expired session: replay the intro?

Cause. sessionExpired() calls signOut(), whose motion.reset() clears the intro flag, so the full 1.2 s launch sequence plays on the Welcome back screen (AppSession.swift, RootView.swift).

Original (today)

Token refused → glow fades up (≈0.4 s) → mark springs in → title and form rise (1.2 s total) → Welcome back, name prefilled.

A · Skip the intro on expiry

Welcome back appears at once, form already shown. The intro still plays after an explicit Sign Out and on first install. Reduce Motion unchanged (already a fade).

B · Short fade (0.3 s)

Keeps a hint of arrival without the long sequence.

Recommendation: A. The person did nothing; the app should not stage a show. One flag: expiry does not call motion.reset().
