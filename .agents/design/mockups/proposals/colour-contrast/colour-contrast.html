Proposal · 2026-10-23 · HIG bucket "Colour & contrast" (17 findings, atlas #144)

Colour that holds 3:1 and never carries meaning alone

Two kinds of finding: glyphs and strokes that fall under 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11; HIG Color and Accessibility), and states shown by colour only (HIG: "don't rely on color alone"). Nothing is built. Everything here is visible, so it waits for the owner's pick. Ratios are computed from the shipped token values (sRGB relative luminance), light and dark.

1 · Measured contrast

Green = ≥4.5:1, yellow = 3–4.5:1, red = under 3:1. Glyph colours are the app's own tokens (HassTokens.Palette, ColorFamily); system colours are the iOS 17 defaults without Increase Contrast. Increase Contrast: system colours darken a step in light mode, but our tokens (Ink.*) are already ≥4.8:1 on the light sheet and do not need a switch; the one place that does switch is the thermostat track (below).

3 · Proposal B: thermostat ring track

Track is label colour at 16% (38% with Increase Contrast): 1.6:1 on dark, 1.45:1 on light; with Increase Contrast 3.5 and 2.6. Options: (1) keep (the coloured trails carry the range; the track is decorative), (2) 42% default, 60% Increase Contrast (≥3.0:1 both appearances; 5.5–6.8:1 with IC), (3) 55% default, 75% IC (heavier ring that competes with the trail). Recommendation: (2). The ring is the control's boundary, so HIG's 3:1 applies; the heavier stroke stays below the 100% trail colours so hierarchy holds. Open question: is a visibly grayer ring acceptable against the current airy look?
