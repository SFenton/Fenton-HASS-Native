Proposal · 2026-10-23

Camera viewer opens at its own height

The camera viewer is the only single-page sheet still forced to the large detent. Security System opens fitted: just its content plus the safe area. The viewer should do the same.

Cause

CameraViewer called .hassSheet(.page, height: .full, appearance: .dark) with the comment "Live video". .full is for sheets whose height must not change while open: tab bars, pushed pages, remotes. The viewer has none of those. Its content is a video and one row of controls, so the large detent leaves about half the sheet empty.

iPhone (402 × 874 pt)

iPad landscape (video beside controls)

What changes in code

FileChange
CameraViewer.swift.hassSheet(.page, height: .full, appearance: .dark) becomes .hassSheet(.page, appearance: .dark). Both layouts go in one ScrollView (the split one had none, so there was nothing for SheetFit to measure).
CameraJourneyTestsThe layout journey asserts the video is not in the top third of the window on iPhone.
Docsmodal-sheet.md, direction-g.md, split-view.md: camera viewer is fitted, no longer "full for live video".

Open points for review

1. Dark fitted sheets skip SheetGroundModifier's opaque ground (only system appearance gets it). The page's own background should cover the sheet, but I have not checked it on iOS 26 glass yet.
2. The camera's own aspect ratio sets the height (Driveway is 10:3, so its sheet is about 100 pt shorter).
