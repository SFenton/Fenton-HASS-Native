Reflow at accessibility sizes

Shrink-to-fit, one-line limits and single-row layouts hold their shape at AX sizes; the proposal reflows them and leaves default sizes alone.

Cause

Six findings are layouts that hold their one-line shape at AX sizes (shrink-to-fit, lineLimit(1), a one-row HStack, an overlay that reserves no room), plus several fixed-size collisions. HIG Typography: reflow, stack and scroll at accessibility sizes rather than truncate. Floating actions already does this (floating-actions.md, owner 2026-10-17); these follow that precedent.

Chip rail at AX5 (status-rail)

ChipRail picks its mode from the size class only (ChipRail.swift:20), so a compact iPhone shows one sideways row of near screen-wide chips; later chips are off screen with no cue.
