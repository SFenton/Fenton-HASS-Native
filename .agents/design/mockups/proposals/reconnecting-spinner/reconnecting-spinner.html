Proposal

Reconnecting, as a quiet arc in the header

The “Reconnecting…” chip under the header goes. A 20 pt white arc spinner takes its place to the left of Quick Links, the same 12 pt from Quick Links as Quick Links is from Profile. It fades in once the connection has been down for 2 s, and fades out when it is live again. No text.

Every header crop on this page is live size (390 pt wide), on the dark wallpaper the signed-in shell always uses. The spinner turns at its real speed (0.82 s a turn).

The sequence

  • 0 to 2 s down: nothing (ReconnectingPolicy.delay, unchanged).
  • 2 s: the arc takes its place in the bar and fades up, 0.35 s ease in-out.
  • Live again: fades down 0.35 s, then the bar item is removed.
  • Reconnect during the fade-out: it fades back up from where it is; it never blinks.
  • Reduce Motion: the arc turns at half speed (as every ArcSpinner does). The fade stays 0.35 s: opacity is not motion. It never freezes.

The spacing

Edge to edge, 12 pt between the arc and Quick Links, the same as the 12 pt measured between Quick Links and Profile on iOS 26. The arc is vertically centred on the 44 pt circles. The two circles do not move when the arc appears, because the trailing group grows to its left.

How it is built

  • On iOS 26 the arc is a bar item with its glass hidden (sharedBackgroundVisibility(.hidden)) so it floats on the wallpaper, the way Profile’s monogram hides its glass. The bar pads a hidden-glass item by 10 pt each side; the arc offsets that so the visible gap lands on 12 pt, then I measure it on the iPhone 17 Pro simulator before this ships.
  • Before iOS 26 the bar draws no glass, so the arc is just placed 12 pt out.

Accessibility

  • The arc is one element labelled “Reconnecting to Home Assistant”, with the updatesFrequently trait and no tap action (ArcSpinner already does this). VoiceOver reaches it in the header, before Quick Links.
  • It is not announced when it appears (a status that comes and goes should not interrupt VoiceOver); a person who swipes to it hears the label.
  • Meaning is carried by shape and motion, not colour. White on the wallpaper keeps contrast above 7:1; the dimmest ring part (22%) is decoration, the 96% and 72% quarters carry it.
  • Large Content Viewer is not needed: the arc is not interactive.

Open questions

1. What happens when offline?

The chip with its words explains why values are stale, and Profile already carries a small cloud-slash badge. Recommend: leave the offline chip as it is for now; it is a different state from a retry, and you did not ask to change it. Say so if you want offline reduced to the badge alone.

2. The 2 s wait

Kept as is. A small arc is less intrusive than the chip, so a shorter wait (1 s) would also be fine. Recommend: keep 2 s, it is already tested against real hand-offs.
