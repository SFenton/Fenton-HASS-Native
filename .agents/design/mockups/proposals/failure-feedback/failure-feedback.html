When a command fails, say so

21 findings share one cause: a rejected or timed-out service call is swallowed. The tile or handle snaps back and nothing says why, and the sign-in extension and Settings "Not Saved" are only visible for 2 s.

Options (shown on a tile, a slider and an AX5 tile)

Pending state

For calls over ~400 ms (lock, garage, alarm, vacuum, device picker): the control keeps its optimistic colour and the trailing glyph becomes a small spinner until HA confirms. Under Reduce Motion the spinner is a static ellipsis glyph. "Not Saved" persists until the next edit or dismiss rather than 2 s.

Recommendation

A with the pending spinner. Extend PendingValue/overlay with a failed(key) state (4 s), expose it through GlassTile.isError (already exists) and a caption slot on slider/dial, and post a VoiceOver announcement plus the .error haptic from the feedback proposal. Update the optimistic-actions pattern doc, which says “no error state between pending and live”; that sentence is the departure this reverses.
