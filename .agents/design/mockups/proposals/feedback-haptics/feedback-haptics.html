Haptics for taps that change the house

Only the thermostat dial, brightness tile, camera viewer and the media pad tick today. Every toggle, lock, garage door, alarm mode, transport key and chore check is silent to touch. HIG Playing haptics: use feedback to confirm an action, and keep it consistent between like controls.

Cause

14 review findings across 15 atlas mockups share one cause: GlassTile (toggle and command tiles), LockTile, GarageDoorTile, AlarmModeButton, the media transport row and volume keys, the 5%-step slider, chore rows, vacuum action buttons, the sign-in failure and the scan sheet have no sensoryFeedback. Existing uses: BrightnessTile.swift:123 (.selection per step), ThermostatDial.swift:90 (.selection), MediaRemoteControls.swift:231 (.impact light, pad only), CameraViewer.swift:130-140 (.impact medium, success/error).

Reduce haptics behaviour

iOS has no per-app Reduce Haptics switch. sensoryFeedback is silenced by the system when Settings → Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics is off, and on devices without a Taptic Engine (iPad, most Duo). So no new in-app setting is proposed. Haptics never carry information alone: every outcome keeps its colour, text and VoiceOver announcement.

Recommendation

Option A. Add one shared modifier in HassDesign (.tapFeedback(_:) wrapping sensoryFeedback with a trigger counter) so every consumer uses one implementation (pattern first). Tests: trigger counter increments per tap and not for navigation tiles; the success/error triggers follow the call result; no UX change for VoiceOver or text size, so the existing 44 pt and AX5 journeys stand.
