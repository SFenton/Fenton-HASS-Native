One convention for alerts and confirmations

The same decision is asked in different words, styles and presenters across the app. This proposes one convention and shows each affected surface as it is today beside the proposal. Nothing is built; the owner picks.

Cause

The mockup atlas review (#144) found 19 findings where one decision is asked two ways, 6 where an irreversible or bulk action asks nothing (or a cheap one asks too much) and 5 where an alert does not say what happened or what to do next. Evidence: Sign Out is an alert titled “Sign Out of Home Assistant?” in the Account sheet (YourSummarySheet.swift) and a confirmationDialog titled “Sign out of Home Assistant?” in Home Assistant settings (HomeAssistantSettingsView.swift); Vacation Mode asks a nine-word title that wraps at larger sizes; Disarm, Turn Off and the vacuum switch carry .destructive on actions the person just chose.

Presenter attachment (dialogs on the Delete button, failure alert split from the Sign Out button, item-based presentation) is behaviour-preserving and is already built or queued as path A on this branch; it needs no decision.

Recommendation

Adopt the table as written. It touches VacationConfig, VacationModeScreen, SecuritySystemSheet, VacuumTabs, VacuumMapPanel, both sign-out sites, the three delete editors, CustomLight*, PerformanceScreen, SoloTripScreen and InventoryItemEditor. Copy moves into one AlertCopy namespace (as LoginCopy does), unit tested for strings and roles, with a UX and an accessibility test (name, role, 44 pt buttons, AX5 stacking) on iPhone, Duo and iPad. No new components.

Open questions

  • Is Sign Out destructive-styled (recommended; it discards the session) or plain?
  • Drop the grocery-removal confirmation, or keep it for consistency with tasks?
  • Should Disarm keep Cancel on the left and a plain Disarm as the bold default?
