> **What:** results of the apple-hig and swiftui-pro skill review of the 30 R1 mockups (tiles, chips, grids, controls, forms, sheets, motion, tokens). **Read when:** you plan fixes to HassDesign or its shared controls. The atlas records findings; no app code was changed.

Totals over 30 mockups: **179 pass, 116 fix, 33 departure** (8 to 12 entries each). Fix severity: 6 medium, 6 medium-low, 1 low-medium, 103 low. Every entry is in its mockup's Reviews section with the rule, file and line. ## Five cross-cutting observations

1. **Hand-built choose-one and sheet chrome.** FilterOptionRow, OptionPickerSheet, SelectableOptionRow and BadgedTabSegments each rebuild a Picker (a Button, checkmark and selected trait), and only the App Icon picker uses HassModal; every other sheet assembles NavigationStack, modalChrome, close item and ground by hand. The patterns exist but are not the call sites' default, and each copy carries its own accessibility wiring.
2. **Feedback and recovery are thin where the house is the source of truth.** No haptic on tiles, no pending state on SegmentedChoice, no retry on the task-load error, no discard confirmation, a cancelled debounce in the date fields. These all fall under HIG Feedback or Modality and cost little to add.
3. **Performance departures are justified but brittle.** HeaderEdgeFade, PagedRow, ChipRail and the ScrollViewportPaging enabler use UIKit probes with measured gains (graph updates 23 to 10 percent; hourly weather 1.1 s to about 0.5 s), recorded as departures. Their shared weakness is view-hierarchy walking; revisit when the iOS 27 observation leak and onScrollGeometryChange cost are fixed.
4. **Dynamic Type is mostly right, with exceptions.** Fixed spinner and swatch sizes, caption and caption2 for state values and badges (one capped at xLarge), shrink-to-fit readouts (thermostat), a capsule radius that stops being round when it grows, and the grid minimums above. Reflow at accessibility sizes (menus, stacked tiles, wrapped capsules) is consistently good.
5. **Colour tokens, strings and tests.** System .green, .orange, .red and .gray bypass HassTokens in StatusPill and InfoBox; palettes define light and dark but no Increased Contrast slot; tokens are built through UIColor closures. The app has no string catalog, so accessibility labels, plurals and joined values are English String literals (low until localization). Positive: contrast, 44 pt targets, Reduce Motion and Reduce Transparency fallbacks are asserted in tests and consistently documented.

## Departures worth knowing (33)

Owner-decided and recorded: trailing circle-x Close on sheets (2026-10-13), icon segments on compact width (2026-10-16), the 0.6 s state fade (2026-10-18), custom arc spinner (2026-10-14), fade-to-clear headers instead of the system scroll edge effect (2026-10-13), tinted translucency instead of materials for tiles (2026-10-17), custom floating search instead of searchable, and own image cache over AsyncImage while the target is iOS 17.
