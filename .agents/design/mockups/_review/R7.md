## Cross-cutting observations

1. **The fold is unhandled in every split.** Seven screens or sheets use equal halves and the mockups said so (food-hub, inventory-space) or drew a hinge band the code lacks (Vacuums, now redrawn). One shared split container on `SplitPolicy.geometry` would close it.
2. **Information hides in hints.** Ingredient shortfalls and disabled-command reasons sit in `accessibilityHint`, which people can switch off; they belong in the value or visible text.
3. **Edit sheets are unguarded.** The shared `FilterSheet`, the item editor and Add Item discard drafts on swipe down, and the editor's Delete skips the confirmation the page uses.
4. **The departures are consistent and recorded.** Circle-X Close, the icon segmented section bar, the glass floating bar, full-height tabbed sheets and non-red Reset each cite an owner decision (2026-10-13 and 2026-10-16); the custom bars still do not move to the Duo vertical bar.
5. **Strong on Dynamic Type, weak on localization.** Tiles, rows, cards and the bar reflow at AX sizes and keep 44 pt targets, but there is no String Catalog in `apple/`, copy is built as `String` (including sentences in HassCore), and several files use UIKit colours.
