## Five cross-cutting observations

1. **Shared primitives carry the accessibility.** GlassTile, BrightnessTile, ModalTabBar and OptionPickerSheet give 44 pt targets, labels, values, adjust actions and Reduce Motion for free; most `pass` entries trace to them. The one systemic flaw is the traits mapping (selection read as a switch).
2. **State by color alone survives where VoiceOver was fixed first.** Power red/green, red/amber severity, the live dot and 3:1 accents on black all have spoken state but a sighted color-blind person gets hue only.
3. **Feedback is uneven.** Haptics exist on the pad, brightness drag and camera outcomes but not on tiles, transport or volume; failures are silent in the camera notification and fade after 2 s on Snapshot. Reduce Motion is handled consistently.
4. **System surfaces fail on capability and privacy, not drawing.** Time-sensitive entitlement, Lock Screen redaction, hidden previews, intent authentication and Siri phrases decide how island, banner and shortcuts behave; the app supplies strings and settings.
5. **UIKit and GCD residue is small and mostly documented.** HorizontalPan, the window probe, the content extension and AnyView in ActivityActions are intentional departures; `DispatchQueue`, `UIColor`, `UIApplication.open` and `UIAccessibility.post` are not.
