## Cross-cutting observations

1. **Security and Climate are not fold-aware.** Home resolves two hinge halves and its rail and grids stop at the fold; Security and Climate render one centred column, so on the unfolded Duo (and iPad, where Security's card stretches) the primary control sits on the fold. Only the standard components are verified; no device run exists for the unfolded poses.
2. **AX5 is handled where the code uses @ScaledMetric and drops where it uses a plain number.** Security doors, Climate contacts, overviews and Weather highlights scale their minimums; ControlTileGrid (guests) and CameraGrid do not. The kit scales every TileGrid, so those two drawings were wrong; they now pass `appMin()`.
3. **Security actions have no touch feedback.** The dial and brightness tile play a selection haptic; alarm modes, locks and garage doors rely on colour, text and a VoiceOver announcement, while Disarm alone gets an alert.
4. **Unavailable is under-reported on cards.** Overview and air-quality cards pass no isUnavailable, so offline sensors read as closed, clear or a reading; page tiles and the pushed detail tiles do dim and say Unavailable.
5. **Departures are documented and consistent**: opaque sheet ground, trailing circle-X Close, flat modules, UIKit paging and chip scroller, fixed white inks on the dark page, full-height sheets. The Close item's `.confirmationAction` placement is the one that is also a semantic mismatch on Duo.
