## Cross-cutting observations
1. **Dismissal and navigation state are not reset by outside events.** Quick Links keeps Close on its root only, and ShellNavigator.open (notifications, Spotlight, shortcuts, Live Activities) leaves a presented Quick Links or Your Summary sheet up over the new page.
2. **State changes are silent to VoiceOver.** The stale-data chip, an expired session, the sign-in arrival and a sign-out in progress post no announcement, focus move or busy state; only login failures are announced (LoginView.swift:88).
3. **The shell is system-first and mostly correct.** TabView, toolbars, NavigationStack, ContentUnavailableView, sheet detents and alerts with roles carry the structure; 145 of 220 entries pass, and the 20 departures all cite an owner decision or a direction-g paragraph.
4. **Deprecated or non-modern API clusters in MainTabView and ShellHeader:** tabItem instead of Tab, toolbar(_:for:) instead of toolbarVisibility, a hand-built Binding(get:set:) for sheets, a device-idiom check for the menu button, and some View computed properties.
5. **Motion and Duo polish.** Literals bypass MotionTokens (summary tabs), arrival sequencing uses Task.sleep, the drawer does not track the finger, close-only sheets do not opt out of the Duo's vertical bar, and LargeDetent reads the first key window.
