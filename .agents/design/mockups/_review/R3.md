## Five cross-cutting observations

1. **Settings screens fail quietly.** Household toggles revert after 2 s, Power Recovery gives no result, the cache and telemetry resets act without asking, the feedback form's blocked-Submit reason is a silent text change, and nothing here plays a haptic. The controller swallows the error in one place (`perform`), so one fix covers admin, guests, special modes and both presence sheets.
2. **Chrome is mostly right and consistent.** Every modal uses `HassModal`/`HouseholdSheet`, a visible grabber, a circle-X Close (owner 2026-10-13, recorded as a departure) and Back on pushes; confirmations are attached to their triggers; the pre-alert location screen follows the HIG rule exactly. The remaining drift is small: toolbar role, UIKit colours, and Sign Out confirmed two different ways.
3. **Contrast was fixed once and not carried through.** Settings, `SettingsSection` and the header/footer inks use white at 85% on the wallpaper after measurements; `FormHeader`/`FormFooter` (Performance, and Images' system header) still use white at 72%, and the Performance rows are opaque system cells beside translucent modules.
4. **Stacked and oversized presentations.** The permission screen opens as a second sheet over Your Summary and is full height for short content; the Home Assistant cover is full screen for a non-media task (departure, AGENTS.md); the pushed web page has no Close. Fold behaviour (hinge, page sheets, the centred sign-in column) is unverified on a device and one case (sign-in, landscape) is known to cross the hinge.
5. **Departures are explicit and mostly sound.** Always-dark pages and sign-in, no two-step code field (owner 2026-10-17), opaque sheet grounds, text Cancel/Submit on the feedback form, web-parity colours and the Charge Status value each cite an owner decision or doc. The location sheet's always-dark rule is now the odd one out next to sheets that follow the system.
