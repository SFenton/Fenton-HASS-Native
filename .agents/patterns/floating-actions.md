# Floating actions and filter sheets

> **What:** page-level floating buttons (Add Task, Add Groceries, Scan Item, recipe/inventory Search, Sort, Filter) and the sort/filter sheets they open. **Read when:** a page has a web `DashboardFloatingAction` or a sort/filter modal.

- One bar per page: `.floatingActionBar([FloatingAction], search: FloatingSearch?)` on the page's scroll view or list (or a container that holds one). It uses `safeAreaBar(edge: .bottom, alignment: .trailing)` with a **soft** bottom scroll edge effect on iOS 26+ (`safeAreaInset` before). Either way the bar's height is added to the scroll view's bottom safe area, so the last row can always scroll clear of the buttons, and rows scrolled under the buttons show through the glass (owner, 2026-10-16: the bar was opaque and the hard edge covered content; HIG Materials: "Let content scroll and peek through while preserving control and navigation legibility"). Never overlay the bar by hand, and never put a second inset under it.
- Direction G (mockups n5 Chores, n6 Recipes and All Food): the bar is the page's bottom toolbar on the navigation layer.
  - `FloatingAction(showsTitle: true)` is a labeled 52 pt capsule. The page's one primary action ("Add Task", "Add Groceries") is **Liquid Glass tinted with G's `#0071E3`** (`isProminent`, the default); a secondary labeled action ("Scan Item" on an empty pantry) passes `isProminent: false` and is untinted glass. Every bar button is `glassEffect(.regular[.tint], in:)` with `.interactive()` on iOS 26 (`BarGlass`), and `.ultraThinMaterial` with the tint laid over it at 88% before iOS 26 (the earlier opaque fill was for contrast at medium detents; the audit passes on glass).
  - `showsTitle: false` is a 52 pt glass circle (Sort, Filter, icon-only Scan) that keeps its title as the VoiceOver label. `isActive` (a sort or filter is applied) tints the glass `#F2F2F7` under a black glyph and adds `.isSelected`.
  - Search (`FloatingSearch`) is a glass capsule that fills the bar's leading space with the magnifier and the prompt (or the typed query); at accessibility sizes it is a magnifier circle. Tapping it opens the field, which takes the bar's width and hides the other actions (web recipe/inventory search). Close clears the text and collapses.
  - Glass is `navigationGlass(in:)` (Liquid Glass on iOS 26, thin material before). Contrast is in `FloatingActionBarTests.tintsKeepTextReadable`; record any other fill the same way. (Before G the bar used the web's chore blue and active brown, darkened to (0,110,145) and (135,95,55).)
- Sort/filter modals use `FilterSheet(title:canApply:canReset:onApply:onReset:)`, an edit sheet in G's form: `SheetCancelItem` (circle x, leading) drops the draft, `SheetConfirmItem("Apply")` (filled check, trailing; `HassDesign/SheetEditToolbar.swift`) commits it and closes, disabled until the draft changes. "Reset to Defaults" is a list row in the action tint that restores defaults in the draft only, shown only while `canReset` (a disabled row fails the contrast audit). Titled sections are `FilterSection("…")`, not `Section("…")` (the system header measured 3.1:1 on the sheet): 15 Semibold in `FormHeader`'s secondary ink. Single-choice options are `FilterOptionRow` (title, secondary subtitle, action-tint checkmark, `.isSelected`); sliders and toggles are plain `Slider` / `Toggle` rows with an `accessibilityValue`. Two to four short options (the recipe filter's 7 / 30 / 90 Days) are a segmented `Picker` with the chosen option's explanation as the section footer, and a `.menu` picker at accessibility sizes (n5 Filter Recipes). A sheet whose count of active filters matters passes `FilterSheet(subtitle:)` ("2 Filters On").
- `FilterSheet(detents:)` defaults to medium and large (large only at AX sizes); pass `[.large]` when the options don't fit the medium height (inventory Sort and Filter), since rows cut by the sheet edge fail the clipped-text audit.
- Consumers (swept 2026-10-16): Chores, Groceries and To-Do (`TodoPageView`, a `List`), Recipes (`RecipesScreen`), Inventory (`InventoryScreen`, a `List`) and the gallery. Each applies the bar to the view that contains its scroll view, never to an `HStack` sibling of a detail pane, so the inset lands on the right column.
- Debug gallery: `FENTON_GALLERY=floating-actions` (journeys in `FloatingActionsJourneyTests`: the last row scrolls clear of the bar and a row under the bar shows through the glass).
