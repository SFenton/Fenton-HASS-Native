# Status rail

> **What:** the horizontal chip row under a page title. **Read when:** adding a summary chip.

- Chips come from config (`HomeConfig.statusChips`) with an entity, symbol, tone and the sheet they open. Pages pass their own subset to `StatusRail(chips:idPrefix:)` (Security shows Security and Contact Sensors).
- `ChipRail` (HassDesign) lays the chips out: compact width (iPhone, folded Duo) scrolls them sideways in one row; regular width (iPad, unfolded Duo) wraps them with `FlowLayout`, so every chip is in view (`ChipRailMode.resolve`). With a window hinge the rail stops 24 pt short of the fold (`ChipRailMetrics.trailingInset`). Home's `StatusRail`, Security's two chips and the room pages' `RoomStatusRail` all use it. Chips keep their own widths so live values never shift neighbors.
- Home has six chips in web order: Lights, Security, Climate, Occupancy, Contact Sensors, Air Quality. Summaries follow the web (`HomeConfig.summary`): Lights and Contact Sensors count their rooms' entities, Occupancy reads Detected/Clear, Air Quality joins the AQI and PM2.5 range helpers. Every chip but Security opens a [room overview sheet](room-overview-sheet.md); Security opens `SecuritySystemSheet`.
- Each chip is a button with the label "<title>, <summary>" for VoiceOver and opens a sheet.
- Chips are pills (`StatusChipButton`, `StatusChipStyle`): a capsule at least 48 pt tall (web `.headerPill`). Owner, 2026-10-13: "Page-level chips are not pills like they are in web or the Apple Home app, and need background colors." Direction G ([direction-g](../design/direction-g.md), "Status chips follow the web"): an active chip fills with its `ColorFamily.chipFill` (a slight top highlight, white title, state at 92% white) and draws its glyph in `chipGlyph`; an idle chip is neutral on the thin `module`. Lights on: amber with an orange glyph. Security: always its mode's fill (`ColorFamily.security(alarmState:)`), neutral while arming or unknown. Climate: always navy `#195482` with the yellow thermometer. Air Quality: always teal (the AQI-coloured glyph is a follow-up). Occupancy and Contact Sensors: green and soft green while occupied or open, neutral otherwise (`StatusChipView.family`, `isFilled`). Room chips map their `RoomTone` to a family (`RoomTone.family`).
- **Scrolling is UIKit's** (owner, 2026-10-16, iPhone 18 Pro Release: "horizontal scrolling on status chips in pages is stutter[y]"). The compact rail is a `UIScrollView` hosting the chips (`HostedHorizontalScroll`, handed the page's environment with `transformEnvironment(\.self)` because a hosting controller starts from a blank one), not a SwiftUI `ScrollView`. Cause: a `ScrollView` nested in a page's `ScrollView` writes its offset through the page's view graph on every frame (`ScrollViewCommitMutation.apply` → `AG::Graph::propagate_dirty`), and that work grows with the size and complexity of the page around it, not with the chips. Time Profiler on the simulator, 25 s of fast swipes (Home's rail over N glass tiles): 59 samples of it with no tiles, 247 with 24, 761 with 120; with the UIKit scroller it is gone and `_updateViewGraph` falls from about 1,200 to 25 samples. The chips were never re-evaluated during a swipe (no body calls), and neither the hinge measurement, the header fade, scroll telemetry nor Buttons were the cause (each removed alone changed nothing). Rules: keep a row that scrolls sideways inside a page out of SwiftUI's `ScrollView`; the other nested horizontal scrolls (weather hourly carousel, suggested recipes, feedback attachments) carry the same cost and move to the same view when they are reported. The hinge inset is measured only in a window that has a hinge (`ChipRailMetrics.appliedInset`), because a window-space frame is recomputed on every frame the page scrolls.
