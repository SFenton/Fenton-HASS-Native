# Room device sheets

> **What:** the device sheets opened from room tiles: bathroom fan, air quality (purifier), humidifier, grill. **Read when:** adding or changing one of them, or a new device sheet that follows the same shape.

Web source: `ha-sfenton-react-dash` `src/components/hass/BathroomFanModalContent.tsx`, `AirPurifierModalContent.tsx`, `HumidifierModalContent.tsx`, `GrillModalContent.tsx` (spec: `plans/room-devices-spec.md` in the project files).

- **Layers.** Config (`HassCore/Config/RoomDeviceConfig.swift`) names the entities per room. One controller per device in `HassCore/RoomDevices/` reads `EntityStore` and sends through `HassServiceCalling` via `RoomDeviceCommands` (optimistic overlay, reset on failure). Views in `HassUI/Features/RoomDevices/` take a controller, so pages and the gallery share them.
- **Chrome.** `RoomDeviceSheetChrome`: NavigationStack, the system inline title with the device status as `navigationSubtitle` on iOS 26+ (the pre-26 fallback is a custom principal title that still shrinks at AX5), the circle-X Close (`device.sheet.done`). Sections are `DeviceSection` (a `RoomSectionHeader(inSheet: true)`) over an equal-height `DeviceTileGrid` of `GlassTile`s in their device's colour family (fan air, grill cooking). Two to four exclusive options are a `SegmentedChoice` (system segmented control, a menu at accessibility sizes; options are `segmentedControls[id].buttons[title]` in UI tests). Cards and pills sit on `HassTokens.Surface.sheetCell`; the one main action per screen is `PrimaryButtonStyle`, secondary buttons are grey bordered capsules, destructive ones plain `Ink.danger` text (direction G). Present with `.hassSheet(.form | .page)` (large detent; page sizing for the humidifier).
- **Optimism.** Fan and purifier clear on the next live change with a 2 s revert (web `useOptimisticState`). The humidifier clears only when Home Assistant confirms the value, with an 8 s revert (web humidifier controller).
- **Fan.** Power and Lock tiles, then the timer: a length menu (5–60 min, default 30) and Set, or a live countdown with Clear. "Auto-disable lock" appears while locked and on; the choice resets when that situation changes. Everything goes through `script.<room>_bathroom_fan_command`.
- **Air quality.** Fan Mode, Auto Mode and Fan Speed are segmented choices; Auto shows the auto sub-modes, Manual shows four speeds (`fan.set_percentage` 0/33/66/100). Subtitle is the AQI / PM2.5 summary.
- **Humidifier.** Mist dial (single-value `ThermostatDial` via `DialValueStyle`, `thermostat-dial.md`) with Turn On/Off; Controls / Schedules / Info is the sheet's bottom `modalTabBar` (`humidifier.tabs`). Two columns (hero beside tabs) only where `SplitPolicy` allows and the window is at least 640 pt wide. Schedules edit the `schedule.*` helper over the websocket (`schedule/list`, `schedule/update`); only admins edit, overlaps block Save, the first saved activity turns scheduling on and deleting the last turns it off.
- **Grill.** State header and four cards: probe sensors read-only with units, Keep Warm and Smoke switches.
- **Accessibility.** Tiles are buttons with on/off or selected values; sensor cards are static text. Dial handles are adjustable ("Mist level", "Target humidity", value "50 percent"). The fan countdown speaks as a duration. Every control is reachable at AX5 by scrolling.
- **Departures from web.** Purifier selections are optimistic (the web waits). Grill switches and humidifier switches send explicit `turn_on` / `turn_off`, never `toggle`. Grill sensors show units. The fan's timer button reads "Set Timer".
- **Not here.** Dishwasher (in the spec, separate lane) and the bathroom fan's room tile with trailing controls (needs the GlassTile slot owned by the Room pages lane). Room pages wire these sheets in.
- **Tests.** Controllers in `HassCoreTests/BathroomFanControllerTests.swift`, `RoomDeviceControllerTests.swift`, `HumidifierControllerTests.swift`, `HumidifierScheduleTests.swift`; dial style in `HassDesignTests/DialValueStyleTests.swift`; hosted renders and gallery echo in `HassUITests/RoomDeviceViewTests.swift`; device journeys, audits and screenshots in `Apps/iOSUITests/RoomDevicesJourneyTests.swift` against `FENTON_GALLERY=room-devices` (`FENTON_GALLERY_DEVICE`, `FENTON_GALLERY_TAB`).
- Header: `RoomDeviceSheetChrome` sets the system inline title and, on iOS 26+, the device status as `navigationSubtitle`, so both follow the bar's Dynamic Type rules. The older custom principal title (headline + caption, `minimumScaleFactor`) shrank at AX5 and failed the humidifier audits; it remains only as the pre-iOS 26 fallback, which still shrinks at AX5 (audit 2026-10-13).
