# Room device sheets

> **What:** the device sheets opened from room tiles: bathroom fan, air quality (purifier), humidifier, grill. **Read when:** adding or changing one of them, or a new device sheet that follows the same shape.

Web source: `ha-sfenton-react-dash` `src/components/hass/BathroomFanModalContent.tsx`, `AirPurifierModalContent.tsx`, `HumidifierModalContent.tsx`, `GrillModalContent.tsx` (spec: `plans/room-devices-spec.md` in the project files).

- **Layers.** Config (`HassCore/Config/RoomDeviceConfig.swift`) names the entities per room. One controller per device in `HassCore/RoomDevices/` reads `EntityStore` and sends through `HassServiceCalling` via `RoomDeviceCommands` (optimistic overlay, reset on failure). Views in `HassUI/Features/RoomDevices/` take a controller, so pages and the gallery share them.
- **Chrome.** `RoomDeviceSheetChrome`: NavigationStack, the system inline title with the device status through `.sheetSubtitle(_:)` (`HassDesign/SheetSubtitle.swift`: the system navigation subtitle on iOS 26+, none earlier), the circle-X Close (`device.sheet.done`). Sections are `DeviceSection` (a `RoomSectionHeader(inSheet: true)`) over an equal-height `DeviceTileGrid` of `GlassTile`s in their device's colour family (fan air, grill cooking). Two to four exclusive options are a `SegmentedChoice` (system segmented control, a menu at accessibility sizes; options are `segmentedControls[id].buttons[title]` in UI tests). Cards and pills sit on `HassTokens.Surface.sheetCell`; the one main action per screen is `PrimaryButtonStyle`, secondary buttons are grey bordered capsules, destructive ones plain `Ink.danger` text (direction G). Present with `.hassSheet(.form | .page)` (large detent; page sizing for the humidifier).
- **Optimism.** Fan and purifier clear on the next live change with a 2 s revert (web `useOptimisticState`). The humidifier clears only when Home Assistant confirms the value, with an 8 s revert (web humidifier controller).
- **Fan** (owner design, 2026-10-17). Power is the air (teal) family and Lock the security blue (`ColorFamily.securityHome`, no new colour), so the tiles read apart at a glance. The timer is a custom segmented row of six lengths (`FanTimerLengthRow`: 5m, 10m, 15m, 20m, 30m, 1h; 44 pt tall; the chosen one is the label's opposite, white with black text on a dark sheet and near-black with white text on a light one, `Surface.selectedSegment`; three columns at accessibility sizes) over one `PrimaryButtonStyle` button, "Turn Off in N Minutes" (or "Turn Off in 1 Hour"), and the footnote "The fan runs, then turns itself off. Starting a timer turns it on if it's off." That copy is true: the HA script's `timer_start` ends with `switch.turn_on` (`/config/scripts.yaml`, checked 2026-10-17), so the button is enabled whenever the fan and timer helper are available and the controller shows the fan on at once. A running timer (`FanTimerRunning`) is a 40 pt timer glyph and a 40 pt semibold monospaced countdown in the web's timer orange (`Ink.timer`: `#FF9F0A` on a dark sheet, `#B35C00` on a light one), no subtitle, a 6 pt orange progress bar that empties as time runs out (`totalSeconds` is the `timer` entity's `duration` attribute, else the shortest offered length that covers what is left), and **Clear Timer** as a red tinted capsule (`TintedCapsuleButtonStyle`). **No "Auto-disable lock"**: the app always sends `auto_unlock: true` and never `timer_auto_unlock`, so the lock is released when the timer ends. The script's own `timer_cancel` leaves the lock as it is (it only clears the auto-unlock flag); that is Home Assistant's behaviour and stays untouched. Everything goes through `script.<room>_bathroom_fan_command`. The sheet is `.hassSheet(.form)`, fitted to its content.
- **Air quality.** Fan Mode, Auto Mode and Fan Speed are segmented choices; Auto shows the auto sub-modes, Manual shows four speeds (`fan.set_percentage` 0/33/66/100). Subtitle is the AQI / PM2.5 summary.
- **Humidifier.** Mist dial (single-value `ThermostatDial` via `DialValueStyle`, `thermostat-dial.md`) with Turn On/Off; Controls / Schedules / Info is the sheet's bottom `modalTabBar` (`humidifier.tabs`). Two columns (hero beside tabs) only where `SplitPolicy` allows and the window is at least 640 pt wide. Schedules edit the `schedule.*` helper over the websocket (`schedule/list`, `schedule/update`); only admins edit, overlaps block Save, the first saved activity turns scheduling on and deleting the last turns it off.
- **Grill.** State header and four cards: probe sensors read-only with units, Keep Warm and Smoke switches.
- **Accessibility.** Tiles are buttons with on/off or selected values; sensor cards are static text. Dial handles are adjustable ("Mist level", "Target humidity", value "50 percent"). The fan countdown speaks as a duration. Every control is reachable at AX5 by scrolling.
- **Departures from web.** Purifier selections are optimistic (the web waits). Grill switches and humidifier switches send explicit `turn_on` / `turn_off`, never `toggle`. Grill sensors show units. The fan's timer button reads "Turn Off in N Minutes" and drops the web's "Auto-disable lock" checkbox.
- **Not here.** Dishwasher (in the spec, separate lane) Room pages wire these sheets in; the bathroom fan's room tile and its Lock and Power rail are in [entity-tile](entity-tile.md).
- **Tests.** Controllers in `HassCoreTests/BathroomFanControllerTests.swift`, `RoomDeviceControllerTests.swift`, `HumidifierControllerTests.swift`, `HumidifierScheduleTests.swift`; dial style in `HassDesignTests/DialValueStyleTests.swift`; hosted renders and gallery echo in `HassUITests/RoomDeviceViewTests.swift`; device journeys, audits and screenshots in `Apps/iOSUITests/RoomDevicesJourneyTests.swift` against `FENTON_GALLERY=room-devices` (`FENTON_GALLERY_DEVICE`, `FENTON_GALLERY_TAB`).
- Header: `RoomDeviceSheetChrome` sets the system inline title and, on iOS 26+, the device status as `navigationSubtitle`, so both follow the bar's Dynamic Type rules. The older custom principal title (headline + caption, `minimumScaleFactor`) shrank at AX5 and failed the humidifier audits (2026-10-13), so it is gone: before iOS 26 the sheet shows the title alone (2026-10-15, shared `.sheetSubtitle`).
