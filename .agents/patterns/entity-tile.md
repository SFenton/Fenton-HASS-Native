# Entity tile

> **What:** the glass tile used for entity controls. **Read when:** showing an entity as a tappable tile.

- `GlassTile(title:subtitle:symbol:family:isActive:)` draws one of direction G's faces (`TileFaceStyle`, [direction-g](../design/direction-g.md) Colour families): **off** is dark (the page module, or `#2C2C2E` in a sheet via `tileSurface`, which `modalChrome()` sets) with the glyph in `ColorFamily.offGlyph`; **lit** is a light that is on, the warm tile with dark ink and a bright band as wide as the brightness; **filled** is any other device that is on, in its family fill with the family ink (progress is a lighter band from the leading edge: with white ink the part still to go is shaded 18% black, never a white band, which measured 4.2:1 under the state on cleaning purple; power's dark ink gets a 25% white band); **error** (`isError:`) is a red glyph and red state on the dense ground, and VoiceOver reads the state. Callers pick a `family`; `tint` is optional and only colours the glyph and rim of the **tinted** face that callers without a family still get (kept so feature files compile unchanged until their G lane picks a family).
- Semantics follow the web UX contract: chevrons only for navigate/modal; every tile is one element with the `.isButton` role (toggles add `.isToggle` and their on/off value). `.accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)` drops the inner `Button`'s role, so `GlassTile` restates it; without it iOS 27 exposes the tile as `Other` and `app.buttons[id]` misses it (found 2026-10-11 on Security); unavailable entities are dimmed and not tappable.
- **Light sheets** ([direction-g](../design/direction-g.md) Layers: Sheets): `GlassTileFace` resolves a `TileAppearance` from `tileSurface` and the environment colour scheme; only a `.sheet` tile under the light scheme is light, and pages are always dark. The off, tinted and error faces then use the white cell, `Ink.primary` titles, `Surface.secondaryInSheet` state ink, the family's `offGlyphRGB(.light)` glyph (a darker variant of the family colour, 3:1 or better on white and the ground), `#C21717` for errors, a 22% wash, and a tint darkened by `TileFaceStyle.lightGlyph(forTint:)`. The lit tile and the filled family faces keep their fixed fills and inks in both appearances. Use `ColorFamily.glyph` and `.offGlyph` (they adapt) for glyphs on a cell or row; use `Color(rgb: family.spec.glyph)` only where the colour is a fixed fill (`PrimaryButtonStyle`).
- **Layout** (`HassDesign/TileLayout`, owner 2026-10-16: "it feels too high up / lots of dead space, but I like the tiles themselves"): `GlassTileFace` draws one of four arrangements chosen by `EnvironmentValues.tileLayout`. The shipped **row** puts the glyph in a fixed 30 pt column and the title and state beside it, the pair vertically centred, with a 48 pt content height (72 pt tile, Apple Home's accessory tile). At accessibility text sizes a row becomes **anchored bottom** (glyph top-leading, text bottom-leading, 72 pt content): a row has no width left for text at AX5. `centeredStack` and the old `stackedTop` stay for the gallery's comparison (Tile Layouts on `FENTON_GALLERY=direction-g`). `BrightnessTile` and every other entity tile draw through the same face, so there is nothing else to keep in step.
- **Chevron.** A `.navigate` tile (or a `BrightnessTile(onOpen:)`) draws its chevron vertically centred on the trailing edge in every layout, and the text keeps `chevronReserve` clear of it. It is no longer in the glyph row.
- **Room tiles** (`fillRGB:`, `TileFaceStyle.colored`): a caller-owned solid fill from `RoomPalette` (the Rooms grid and Climate's Advanced Controls rooms). White ink and a filled white glyph on or off, flat (no top highlight), the same in both appearances. Error and unavailable still win. Filled family tiles (on) already draw the fill symbol variant in white, so a device that is on reads as a filled white glyph; off tiles keep the outline family glyph.
- **Navigation tiles reserve two title lines** (`.tileLayout(.anchoredBottom).tileTitleLines(2)`, `TileMetrics.minContentHeight`; owner, 2026-10-16: "Rooms should have taller tiles with room for two lines of text"). The Rooms grid, Climate's Rooms tab and the Quick Links grid use it with `DynamicGridLayout(equalRowHeights: true)` and `.equalHeightTiles()`: the glyph top-leading, the name on the bottom line (a one-line name sits on the bottom line, a two-line name fills both), one height for every tile (glyph + 8 pt + two lines of subheadline + padding, 96 pt at the default size, from `ScaledMetric`s so it grows with Dynamic Type). `.anchoredBottom` has no stack spacing, so the spacer's 8 pt minimum is the whole gap and the reserved height is the drawn height. Device tiles on room pages keep the Row.
- **Quick Links tiles** (`QuickLinkPalette`, `GlassTile(fillRGB:)`): solid fill, filled white glyph, white ink, one colour per link from its own page's family (Rooms is the one new navy). Quick Links was unequal because it had no `equalRowHeights`: with the Row layout (#95) a tile with a state line was taller than one without.
- **Finishes** (`HassDesign/TileFinish`, `tileFinish(_:)`): three frosted proposals beside the shipped `.solid` finish, in the Direction G gallery's Tile Finish section only (nothing ships one until the owner picks). They differ in `FrostedGround` (the ground: underlay, white veil, translucent fill, one sheen gradient) and `FrostedRim` (a 1 pt hairline lit at the top, B's coloured edge), never a live blur or material. Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast resolve to `.solid` (`TileFinish.effective`). Dark-ink faces (lit, power) stay opaque in every finish. `FENTON_TILE_FINISH` (Debug) sets the default for the scroll cost probe. See direction-g.md, Tile finish.
- Tile size stays stable while values update.
- Disabled (`.disabled`) tiles keep full-contrast text: `GlassTileButtonStyle` never fades the label, and the face mutes only the icon (secondary) and border. A locked tile still carries state ("Away", "Select the user … first"); `.plain` faded it to 2.5:1 and failed the contrast audit (Solo Trip, iPhone, 2026-10-11). VoiceOver still reads it as dimmed.
- Unavailable (`isUnavailable`) tiles follow the same rule: the face halves the opacity of the glyph and the tinted stroke only. The title, the `Unavailable` value and the ground stay at full strength, because dimming the ground lets the wallpaper show through behind white text. A whole-face fade left the text at 2.4:1, and the signed-in Security tab failed its audit on the folded Duo before states arrived (2026-10-14). `AlarmModeButton` matches. The Security gallery's `FENTON_GALLERY_SCENARIO=unavailable` audits it.
- Every entity tile draws through `GlassTileFace`; `BrightnessTile` (`HassDesign/BrightnessTile.swift`, web `LightBrightnessCard.tsx`) is the dimmable-light variant. Tap flips power, a horizontal drag sets brightness from the finger's position and commits on release, a vertical drag scrolls. The fill is the light's `rgb_color` (else the light tint) as a 32% wash so labels keep contrast. VoiceOver: activate flips power, adjust steps 10% (the web keyboard steps 5%; 10% keeps VoiceOver swipes practical). Value reads `Off`, `45%` or `Unavailable`. On iOS 18+ the drag is a UIKit pan (`HorizontalPan`) that only begins on a mostly horizontal movement. A SwiftUI `DragGesture`, even attached as a simultaneous gesture, stops a parent `ScrollView` from scrolling when the drag starts on a tile (found 2026-10-11: Custom Lights could not scroll to Reset All Lights). Gesture and optimistic state live in the pure `BrightnessInteraction` value type so tap, drag and adjust are unit-tested; the view only wires gestures and the pending timeout to it.
- The dragger (direction G): `BrightnessTile` draws the lit face. While a horizontal drag is in progress the tile lifts (scale 1.03 and a shadow; shadow only with Reduce Motion), a `BrightnessBubble` ("30%") floats over the band's edge, kept inside the tile and hidden from VoiceOver, and `.sensoryFeedback(.selection)` ticks each time the drag crosses a 10% step (`hapticStep`).
- `BrightnessTile(onOpen:)` makes a tap open the light's details instead of flipping power (web `tapAction: 'more-info'`, Custom Lights): the tile shows a chevron, VoiceOver's activate opens details and a "Turn On"/"Turn Off" custom action flips power; drag and adjust still set brightness.
- Light power is always an explicit `turn_on` / `turn_off` from the shown state via `LightControl` (`HassCore/Lights`), never `toggle`: Presence Based Lighting's Presence Lock reverts toggles in empty rooms and only honors a bare `turn_on` from an app user. Brightness 0 sends `turn_off`.
- Kinds (`GlassTileKind`): `.toggle` (`.isToggle`, On/Off), `.navigate` (chevron), `.command`, `.selection(isSelected:)` (`.isSelected` while chosen, web `aria-pressed`), `.state` (read-only: not a button, never dimmed as disabled).
- A tile with an accessory takes its identifier through `.tileIdentifier(_:)`, never `.accessibilityIdentifier` on the whole tile: that one also lands on the accessory's buttons and hides their identifiers (bathroom fan Lock and Power, 2026-10-12).
- Optional `wash` (a state color over the material, e.g. towel rack on, bed heating), `progress` / `progressTint` (leading fill, e.g. dishwasher), and a trailing `accessory` view builder for in-tile controls (bathroom fan lock and power) that sit outside the tile's button; the face keeps room for it.
- A tappable tile is a `GlassTile` with its own `action` (push through a `NavigationStack` path when it opens a page). Never put a tile drawn with `.allowsHitTesting(false)` inside a `Button` or `NavigationLink` label: a label with no hit-testable content leaves the outer control untappable (Quick Links and the room grid ignored every tap on iPhone, 2026-10-11). `.allowsHitTesting(false)` is only for read-only faces (`MediaRemoteTabs` state tiles).
- `GlassStateTile` (feature-local in `HassUI/Features/Settings/Household`; the shared read-only kind is now `GlassTile(kind: .state)`, so new read-only tiles use that) draws the same face with no action, for values with no action (web `EntityActionCard` without an action, e.g. Mach-E). VoiceOver reads the title and the shown state.
- Put a tappable tile's accessibility label, value and traits on its `Button` directly, never behind `.accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)`: that wrapper leaves the Button exposed beside a second element, so an identifier set by the caller, the value and the disabled state land on the non-button one and `app.buttons[id]` finds nothing (found 2026-10-11 on the household Settings tiles). `SelectableOptionRow` follows the same rule.
- Thermostat and bed-side tiles take their face from `ThermostatTileFace` (`HassDesign`), the one rule for both: heating and cooling are filled; idle or off is the off face with the Heating glyph (heat mode, or a bed level of zero or more) or the Cooling glyph (any other mode, or a negative bed level), never neutral grey (2026-10-15). `RoomTileFace.resolve` (room cards) and `BedroomStyle.face` (`BedSideTile`) both call it; a new thermostat or bed tile must too. Read-only temperature sensor tiles (the Climate chip sheet) are not thermostats and keep the `.climate` family.
