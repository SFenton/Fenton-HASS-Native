# Modal sheet

> **What:** how web hash modals become native sheets. **Read when:** presenting a modal.

- Web hash routes (`#lights-overview`) map to a `HomeSheet` enum driving `.sheet(item:)`. The URL hash is not needed natively.
- Container: `HassDesign/HassModal.swift`. New modals use `HassModal(title, closeIdentifier:) { content }`; existing ones put `SheetCloseItem { dismiss() }` in their toolbar and `modalChrome()` on their root.
- Close: the circle-X `SheetCloseItem` at the top trailing edge (`Button(role: .close)` on iOS 26), never a text "Done" or "Close" (owner, 2026-10-13, matching FestivalNativeApps; HIG Toolbars: "Close dismisses a modal; prefer their standard symbols without text labels"). A sheet that saves keeps its Save or Add button; Cancel stays only where it discards an edit.
- Create and edit sheets (direction G, Sheets): `SheetCancelItem` (circle x, leading) and `SheetConfirmItem("Save", isEnabled:isBusy:)` (a check on a filled `#0071E3` circle, trailing, with an inline `ArcSpinner` while saving), both in `HassDesign/SheetEditToolbar.swift`, as in Calendar and Reminders. Never text Cancel/Save buttons and never a second copy of these.
- Header: no translucent band; content fades out under it (`modalChrome()`, [header-edge-fade](header-edge-fade.md)). Each page pushed inside a modal applies `modalChrome()` too. Modals have no house backdrop ([page-backdrop](page-backdrop.md)).
- Height: every modal opens full height on iPhone with the grabber visible (`.hassSheet()`; owner, 2026-10-13: "All modals ... need to open to full height on iPhone"). iPad shows its form or page sheet.
- **iPad size (plan Phase 4, 2026-10-15).** Every sheet names it: `.hassSheet(.page)` for viewing and control sheets with tile grids, dials, maps or a second column (chip sheets, Security System, Weather, Quick Links, room card sheets, Sprinklers, Custom Light, remotes, thermostat, vacuum, recipe detail, camera); `.hassSheet(.form)` for lists, editors and prompts (light mode picker, Vacation and Solo Trip editors, Recipes filters, Bed temperature scope, Add Item, todo editors, filter and sort sheets). `HassSheetWidth` has two cases and the default is `.form`: there is no `.automatic`, so a sheet that forgets still gets a narrow readable sheet instead of the system's default, and the rule above decides when to ask for `.page`. A sheet that keeps its own detents (the todo prompt at the medium detent, `FilterSheet` with `detents`) applies only the size with `hassSheetSizing(_:)`. A sheet that bypasses `hassSheet` for its own reason (the item editor's grabber that hides while a save blocks dismissal) still routes through it and overrides the one modifier after.
- Appearance (owner decision, 2026-10-15): pages stay dark on the wallpaper, sheets follow the system appearance. `hassSheet()` and `HassModal` apply `followsSystemAppearance()` (the scheme `RootView` publishes as `systemColorScheme`, because the shell's `preferredColorScheme(.dark)` hides it from the environment); a sheet over a photo or gradient says `hassSheet(appearance: .dark)` (Weather, the camera viewer). Never write `.preferredColorScheme` in a feature sheet otherwise, and give every sheet `hassSheet()` or `HassModal`, or it stays dark. Colour sheet content only with the adaptive tokens (`Ink.*`, `Surface.sheetGround/sheetCell/secondaryInSheet/separator`, `ColorFamily.glyph`): no `.white`, `Color.black`, `Palette.*` or `Surface.secondaryOnModule` on a view a sheet shows, except text on a fixed fill. A journey renders sheets in light with `FENTON_APPEARANCE=light` (`launchEnvironment`, Debug builds) and audits them with `FentonHomeApp.audit`.
- A sheet that navigates (room → lights) pushes inside its own `NavigationStack` with Back, matching the web's in-sheet back.
- A tabbed sheet (media remote, thermostat, vacuum, recipe detail) uses `HassUI/Common/ModalTabBar.swift`, pinned along the bottom with `.modalTabBar(...)` after `modalChrome()` (direction G; `placement: .top` remains for a sheet whose lane has not moved yet): a system segmented control on a glass capsule (`navigationGlass`), of text or of icons when the caller passes `symbol:` (the vacuum, whose labels are too long), and a menu at accessibility sizes. Never a second tab control.
- Why a segmented control on glass at the bottom (direction G, owner-approved mockups, 2026-10-13): it stays a segmented control, not a tab bar, because HIG Segmented controls (iOS, iPadOS) say "Consider it for switching between closely related subviews; use a tab bar for completely separate app sections", and a modal's sections are related views of one device. The glass places it on the navigation layer floating over the content, like the web's bottom `ModalIconTabNav` (HIG: Materials, Liquid Glass). Segments hold "text or images, not a mix", about 5 on iPhone. To change a tab's content without a hard cut, drive the panel with `HassUI/Common/SmoothContentSwap.swift` (fade out, swap while hidden, fade in; Reduce Motion swaps at once); the vacuum sheet does.
